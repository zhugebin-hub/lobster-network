## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Content — Large calligraphic quote
Narrative Goal: Present the core teaching of the entire sutra in its most famous phrase.

Key Content:
- Headline: 第三品 · 大乘正宗分
- Central quote (very large, brush calligraphy): 应无所住而生其心
- Body below: 度一切众生，而实无众生可度
- Body below: 破除我相、人相、众生相、寿者相

Visual: The central phrase "应无所住而生其心" in very large brush calligraphy, dominating the upper half. Bold ink brush stroke sweeping diagonally behind it. Below, two explanatory lines in smaller clean serif. Soft sage green watercolor wash as background accent.

Layout: Quote centered upper two-thirds. Explanatory text below. Single sweeping brush stroke as the key visual element.
