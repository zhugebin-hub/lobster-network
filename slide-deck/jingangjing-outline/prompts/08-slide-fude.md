## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash, zen minimalism.
Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with watercolor paper texture.
Typography: Headlines — Chinese brush calligraphy. Body — clean serif.
Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), soft coral (#F4A261), sage green (#87A96B), pale gold (#D4A84B).
Visual Elements: Comparison arrows, watercolor scale imagery, ink wash accents.
Density: Balanced — comparison layout.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Content — Comparison layout
Narrative Goal: Contrast worldly merit (七宝布施) with dharma merit (受持般若).

Key Content:
- Headline: 第十一品 · 无为福胜分 / 第十二品 · 尊重正教分
- Left side title: 有为福报
- Left body: 七宝布施 — 短暂有漏，终有尽时
- Right side title: 无为福报
- Right body: 受持般若、悟无住无相 — 无漏大福，无量倍胜
- Bottom note: 无论在家出家，受持此经，一切天人皆应供养

Visual: Two-column comparison. Left side — small pile of treasures (gold, jewels) rendered in soft watercolor, with a fading arrow showing impermanence. Right side — an open sutra scroll rendered in sage green and pale gold wash, with a rising arrow showing limitless merit. Between them, a zen brush stroke arrow pointing from left to right. The right side is visually larger/more prominent.

Layout: Headline at top. Comparison columns below with central arrow. Bottom note spanning both columns.
