## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash, zen minimalism.
Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with watercolor paper texture.
Typography: Headlines — Chinese brush calligraphy. Body — clean serif.
Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), soft coral (#F4A261), sage green (#87A96B), pale gold (#D4A84B), sky blue (#7EC8E3).
Visual Elements: Four soft cards with watercolor wash backgrounds, lotus motifs, ink brush accents.
Density: Balanced — four concepts in card layout.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Content — Four-card layout
Narrative Goal: Teach that even spiritual attainments are empty of inherent existence; purifying mind purifies land.

Key Content:
- Headline: 第九品 · 一相无相分 / 第十品 · 庄严净土分
- Card 1: 四果假名 — 须陀洹至阿罗汉，皆无实有可得
- Card 2: 果位只是假名 — 不可执着修行果位之相
- Card 3: 庄严佛土 — 心净则国土净，非外求可得
- Card 4: 即非庄严 — 庄严佛土者，即非庄严，是名庄严

Visual: Four soft rectangular cards arranged in a 2×2 grid. Each card has a different subtle watercolor wash background: soft coral, sage green, pale gold, sky blue. Card titles in brush calligraphy style. Body text in clean serif. Small lotus petal icon in the corner of each card.

Layout: Headline at top. 2×2 card grid below with even spacing.
