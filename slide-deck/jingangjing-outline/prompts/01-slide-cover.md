## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash (水墨画) influences. Visible brush strokes, ink bleeds, negative space (留白). Zen minimalism meets warm watercolor.

Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with subtle watercolor paper texture. Faint ink wash mountain silhouette at bottom.

Typography: Headlines — elegant Chinese brush calligraphy style. Body — clean serif for readability.

Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), soft coral (#F4A261), sage green (#87A96B), pale gold (#D4A84B), pale ink wash (#E8E4DC).

Visual Elements: Ink wash mountain silhouettes, watercolor lotus motifs, zen brush strokes, generous negative space (留白).

Density: Minimal — breathing room is essential. Each slide conveys ONE clear message.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Cover
Narrative Goal: Title slide — establish zen atmosphere, introduce the Diamond Sutra overview.

Key Content:
- Headline (large, centered, brush calligraphy style): 金刚般若波罗蜜经
- Sub-headline (smaller, below): 三十二品整体脉络
- Small bottom text: 水墨禅风讲义

Visual: Centered title on warm off-white background. Faint ink wash mountain landscape along the bottom edge. Single watercolor lotus bloom, subtle and soft, near the bottom right corner. Generous negative space (留白) around the title — the empty space IS the design.

Layout: Centered vertical composition. Title occupies upper-middle third. Mountains form a gentle horizon at the bottom 20%. Lotus bloom adds a touch of color in the bottom right.
