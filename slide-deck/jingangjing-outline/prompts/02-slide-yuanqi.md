## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash influences, zen minimalism.
Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with watercolor paper texture.
Typography: Headlines — Chinese brush calligraphy style. Body — clean serif.
Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), soft coral (#F4A261), sage green (#87A96B), pale gold (#D4A84B).
Visual Elements: Ink wash brush strokes, lotus motifs, zen circles (圆相), negative space (留白).
Density: Minimal to balanced.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Content
Narrative Goal: Introduce the opening scene — Buddha's daily life as the setting for this great teaching.

Key Content:
- Headline: 第一品 · 法会因由分
- Body point 1: 佛陀日常乞食、洗足、敷座而坐
- Body point 2: 行住坐卧皆是般若道场
- Body point 3: 以平常心示现，于日常中见佛法

Visual: Left side — text content with zen spacing. Right side — ink wash illustration of a simple alms bowl (钵) with soft watercolor wash. A single zen circle (圆相) drawn with visible brush strokes behind the bowl. Soft sage green accent wash behind the text area.

Layout: Two-column split. Text left (60%), illustration right (40%).
