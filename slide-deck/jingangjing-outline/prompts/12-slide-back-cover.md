## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash, zen minimalism.
Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with subtle watercolor paper texture. Minimal ink wash at bottom.
Typography: Headlines — Chinese brush calligraphy. Body — clean serif, small.
Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), pale gold (#D4A84B), sage green (#87A96B).
Visual Elements: Minimalist ink wash, single zen brush stroke, generous negative space (留白).
Density: Minimal — maximum breathing room.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Back Cover
Narrative Goal: Closing — leave the audience with the spirit of receiving and practicing the teaching.

Key Content:
- Main text (centered, brush calligraphy): 信受奉行
- Sub-text (smaller, below): 大众闻法开悟，信受奉行
- Small bottom text: 金刚般若波罗蜜经 · 三十二品脉络讲义

Visual: "信受奉行" in large brush calligraphy, centered in the upper half. Below it, a single horizontal zen brush stroke (一) in soft ink wash. Very faint ink wash mountain at the bottom edge — mirroring the cover but even more subtle. A single watercolor lotus petal, soft and fading, in the bottom left corner. Massive negative space (留白) — the emptiness carries meaning.

Layout: Centered vertical composition. Text upper-middle. Brush stroke below. Mountains at very bottom. Everything else is empty space.
