## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash, zen minimalism.
Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with watercolor paper texture.
Typography: Headlines — Chinese brush calligraphy style, large and impactful. Body — clean serif.
Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), soft coral (#F4A261), sage green (#87A96B).
Visual Elements: Ink wash brush strokes, question mark rendered as zen brush stroke, negative space.
Density: Minimal — let the questions breathe.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Content — Quote-style layout
Narrative Goal: Present the two central questions that drive the entire sutra.

Key Content:
- Headline: 第二品 · 善现启请分
- Quote (large, centered, calligraphic): 善男子善女人，发菩提心者，应云何住？云何降伏其心？
- Sub-text: 全经问答，由此一问展开

Visual: Large central question rendered in brush calligraphy style. Two question marks (？？) stylized as zen brush strokes with ink drip effect. Soft pale gold wash behind the question text to make it stand out. Faint lotus petal shapes scattered at the edges.

Layout: Quote centered in upper two-thirds. Sub-text smaller, below. Generous negative space around the question.
