## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash, zen minimalism.
Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with watercolor paper texture.
Typography: Headlines — Chinese brush calligraphy. Body — clean serif.
Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), soft coral (#F4A261), sage green (#87A96B).
Visual Elements: Dissolving/evaporating ink forms, fading shapes, negative space.
Density: Balanced.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Content — Split layout
Narrative Goal: Teach that all appearances are illusory — form dissolves into emptiness.

Key Content:
- Headline: 第五品 · 如理实见分 / 第六品 · 正信希有分
- Quote (large): 凡所有相，皆是虚妄。若见诸相非相，即见如来。
- Body point 1: 一切外在形相，都非真实本体
- Body point 2: 不执着四相：我相、人相、众生相、寿者相
- Body point 3: 般若空义难信稀有，受持此经消无量业障

Visual: Left side — text content. Right side — ink wash illustration showing a solid form (a circle or mountain) gradually dissolving/fading into empty space, symbolizing "诸相非相". Soft watercolor bleeding effect where the form dissolves. Pale ink wash gradient from solid to nothing.

Layout: Split — text left (55%), dissolving ink illustration right (45%).
