## STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS

Design Aesthetic: 水墨禅风 — Soft watercolor with Chinese ink wash, zen minimalism.
Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F0) with full-bleed watercolor ink wash landscape.
Typography: Headlines — very large Chinese brush calligraphy. Body — clean serif, smaller.
Color Palette: Ink black (#2D2D2D), warm charcoal (#3D3D3D), soft coral (#F4A261), sage green (#87A96B), pale gold (#D4A84B), sky blue (#7EC8E3).
Visual Elements: Full ink wash landscape background, dreamlike quality, dewdrop and bubble motifs.
Density: Minimal — let the verse breathe.

## SLIDE CONTENT

Type: Highlight — Large centered verse
Narrative Goal: Present the most famous verse of the Diamond Sutra — the core teaching.

Key Content:
- Small top label: 第三十二品 · 应化非真分
- Central verse (very large, brush calligraphy, centered):
  一切有为法
  如梦幻泡影
  如露亦如电
  应作如是观
- Small bottom note: 说法不取诸相，如如不动

Visual: The four-line verse in large brush calligraphy, centered and dominating the slide. Behind it, a full-bleed ink wash landscape — faint mountains shrouded in mist, giving a dreamlike quality. Small watercolor bubbles and dewdrops scattered around the edges, rendered in pale gold and sky blue wash, symbolizing "梦幻泡影" (dreams, illusions, bubbles, shadows). The overall feeling is ethereal and transcendent.

Layout: Top label small. Verse large and centered. Bottom note small. Background landscape fills the entire slide with soft ink wash.
