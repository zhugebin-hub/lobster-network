## Speech Text

Good morning, everyone!

Today, I'd like to share my experiment comparing two types of AI assistants on DingTalk.

First, I tested DingTalk's Native AI Assistant — the built-in AI powered by Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen model. It's ready to use immediately, with deep integration into DingTalk's ecosystem. I tested it with six scenarios: greetings, knowledge questions, task handling, professional consulting, multi-turn conversations, and creative writing. It scored 4.9 out of 5 — excellent performance across all areas.

Second, I tested a Self-Built AI Assistant — created using Alibaba Cloud's Bailian platform with customizable knowledge bases. While it performed well in specialized domains like our "Crayfish Assistant" for aquaculture questions, it scored only 2.5 out of 5 overall due to limited general knowledge and no context memory.

Key Findings:

One — Native AI excels in conversation fluency, knowledge breadth, and user experience.

Two — Self-Built AI offers better data control and customization for specific business needs.

Three — Best Solution: A hybrid approach — use native AI for general tasks, and self-built AI for specialized company knowledge.

Conclusion: For most students and small businesses, DingTalk's native AI is sufficient. But for enterprises with proprietary knowledge, building your own AI assistant is worth the effort.

Thank you!

## 中文翻译

大家早上好！

今天我想分享一个对比钉钉两种 AI 助手的实验。

首先，我测试了钉钉原生 AI 助理——这是内置的 AI，基于阿里通义千问大模型。它开箱即用，与钉钉生态深度集成。我测试了六个场景：问候、知识问答、任务处理、专业咨询、多轮对话和创意写作。它获得了 4.9 分（满分 5 分）——所有场景表现优秀。

其次，我测试了自建 AI 助手——使用阿里云百炼平台创建，可配置自定义知识库。虽然在专业领域（比如我们的"小龙虾助手"）表现不错，但总体只得了 2.5 分，因为通用知识有限且没有上下文记忆。

主要发现：

第一，原生 AI 在对话流畅度、知识广度和用户体验方面表现优异。

第二，自建 AI 在数据控制和特定业务定制方面更有优势。

第三，最佳方案是混合使用——通用任务用原生 AI，专业公司知识用自建 AI。

结论：对大多数学生和小企业，钉钉原生 AI 已经够用。但对于有专有知识的企业，自建 AI 助手值得投入。

谢谢！
