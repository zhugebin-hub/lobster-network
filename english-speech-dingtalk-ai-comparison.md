Today, I'd like to share my experiment comparing two types of AI assistants on DingTalk.

**First, I tested DingTalk's Native AI Assistant** — the built-in AI powered by Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen model. It's ready to use immediately, with deep integration into DingTalk's ecosystem. I tested it with six scenarios: greetings, knowledge questions, task handling, professional consulting, multi-turn conversations, and creative writing. **Second, I tested a Self-Built AI Assistant** — created using Alibaba Cloud's Bailian platform with customizable knowledge bases. **Key Findings:**

1. **Native AI** excels in conversation fluency, knowledge breadth, and user experience
2. **Self-Built AI** offers better data control and customization for specific business needs
3. **Best Solution:** A hybrid approach — use native AI for general tasks, and self-built AI for specialized company knowledge

**Conclusion:** For most students and small businesses, DingTalk's native AI is sufficient. But for enterprises with proprietary knowledge, building your own AI assistant is worth the effort.
